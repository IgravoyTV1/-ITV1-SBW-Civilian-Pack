## 🇬🇧 ENG

An extension for the **Superb Warfare** mod, introducing civilian vehicles and city transport. Focused on iconic Soviet and Russian automotive classics, this addon is built for **Roleplay servers** and custom modpacks. All models and optimized textures are custom-built from scratch for a cohesive style without hurting performance.

### ✨ Key Features (v1.0.0)

* 🚗 **VAZ-2107 "Zhiguli"** — angular sedan with 4 working seats, functional trunk, horn and authentic ~150 km/h top speed
* 🎨 **11 factory colors**, fully compatible with the base mod's repainting

### 🗺️ Roadmap

* 🚙 Soviet & Russian fleet: VAZ-2105, VAZ-2106, UAZ-469, UAZ-452 "Bukhanka", heavy trucks
* 🌍 Foreign icons from various eras
* 🚓 Specials: police, taxi, tuned versions
* 🖌️ New liveries and paint options

### ⚙️ Requirements

* Minecraft 1.21.1 + NeoForge
* Superb Warfare 0.8.9+
* License: GPL-3.0
